# OpsAtlas

OpsAtlas is a public, English-language IT knowledge base for administrators who know the products they manage but may need a reliable reminder of a command, portal path, diagnostic step, or its purpose. It is also intended as a reusable community reference.

Articles grow from real solved cases. There is no prewritten topic catalog: a topic folder is added only when an article needs it. Read articles directly on GitHub; a documentation website is outside the current scope.

## Content types

- **Notes** capture short, practical knowledge without forcing a full procedure.
- **Explanations** clarify a concept or mechanism and when it matters.
- **Guides** walk through a diagnosis or change, explain why relevant steps are taken, and show how to interpret and verify the result.

The [templates](templates/) provide starting points; use only sections that fit the case. For the boundaries of the project, see [Project scope](PROJECT_SCOPE.md). To turn a solved case into a safe, reusable article, see [Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md).
